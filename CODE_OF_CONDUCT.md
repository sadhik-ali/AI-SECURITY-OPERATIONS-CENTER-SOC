# Code of conduct

A short version of the Contributor Covenant, adapted for a project whose subject
matter is security.

## Expectations

Be direct about technical problems and generous about people. Review the code, not
the author. Assume good faith, and say so when you do not have enough context to
assume anything.

Because this is a security tool, three things are explicitly part of conduct, not
off-topic:

- **Do not publish exploit paths for a live deployment of this project.** Report
  them privately through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Do not share real telemetry, credentials, or victim data** in issues, PRs,
  screenshots, or chat. Synthetic fixtures exist for a reason; RFC 5737
  documentation ranges are the default here.
- **Do not pressure for an automated-response feature** on the grounds that it
  would be convenient. Human-in-the-loop is the position of this project, and
  disagreement with it is fine — but it is not a conduct exemption.

Harassment, exclusionary behaviour, and personal attacks are not acceptable in any
project space: issues, PRs, discussions, or direct messages that concern the
project.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer through the contact on the repository's GitHub profile, or
open a private security advisory if the concern is also a vulnerability. Reports
are handled by someone other than the subject when that is possible; the reporter's
identity is kept private.

## Consequences

A maintainer may edit, close, or remove comments, issues, or commits that violate
this policy, and may temporarily or permanently bar a contributor from the project.
Serious or sustained violations end participation.

This policy applies in project spaces and in public spaces where someone is
representing the project.
